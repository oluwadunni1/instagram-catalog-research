ICatalog-IG

Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026 · Terms of Service

ICatalog-IG is a non-commercial research proof of concept operated by Dunni Olu-Ajayi ("we"). It turns the posts of an Instagram professional account into a structured product catalog: product names, prices, and signals such as "sold" or "out of stock". This policy explains what the app reads from Instagram, what it does with it, and how to have it deleted.

Who the app serves

The app is not offered to the public. It connects only to Instagram professional accounts that we own or manage, through Instagram Login, and each account owner authorises it explicitly. No one else can sign in to it.

What we read from Instagram

From each connected account, through Meta's Instagram API:

  • Profile: account id, username, name, biography, account type and post count.
  • Posts: captions, timestamps, media type, image and video links, thumbnails, permalinks, carousel items and comment counts.
  • Comments on those posts: comment id, text, the commenter's username, timestamp and like count.

The app does not read direct messages, publish or edit content, or read any account that has not authorised it. It reads comments only on the connected account's own posts.

How we use it

  • To extract products, prices and availability signals from posts and their comments.
  • To detect which posts changed between two pulls, so unchanged posts are not processed again.
  • To measure how accurately the app does both, against labels we write by hand.

We do not sell this information, use it for advertising, build profiles of commenters, or contact anyone based on it.

Services that process it for us

Captions, comments and post images are sent to these AI services to be read, and each processes them under its own terms:

  • OpenRouter, which passes requests to Google's Gemini models.
  • TypeSafe, which runs the Jev classification model.
  • Google AI Studio, which turns captions into numeric embeddings used for change detection.

Images are also downloaded to our own computer for text recognition and image fingerprinting, which run locally.

Where it is stored

  • On our own computer, and in private cloud storage (Cloudflare R2) that only we can access.
  • The project's source code is public on GitHub, together with post captions and metadata from our own connected accounts. Comment text and commenters' usernames are never published there.

How long we keep it

For as long as the research project runs. When it ends, we delete the Instagram data we hold. You can ask us to delete it sooner (see below).

Deleting your data

If you own a connected account: remove the app in Instagram under Settings → Apps and websites, which stops all further access, and email us at dunnioluajayi@gmail.com with your username to have what we already hold deleted.

If you commented on a connected account's post: deleting the comment on Instagram stops us reading it again. To have a copy we already hold deleted, email dunnioluajayi@gmail.com with your username and the post's link.

We delete the data within 30 days of a request and reply to confirm it.

Cookies and tracking

These pages and the app use no cookies, analytics or tracking.
